A taste of what you'll learn

The strangler fig

It's one of the most fascinating trees in the cloud forest. It starts as a seed a bird leaves high up on another tree, and grows downward until it reaches the ground. Over the years it wraps around its host completely. On the tour I'll show you several and tell you the whole story right in front of them.
